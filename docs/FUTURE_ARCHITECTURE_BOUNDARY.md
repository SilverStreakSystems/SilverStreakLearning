# Future Architecture Boundary

The Future Architecture view shows nine target-state workspaces. Every one is
rendered inside a gate that states, in text: "Target-state candidate — not
current implementation authority."

Rules:

- Gated concepts hold Gated Future Concept authority and never grant
  implementation authority.
- A gated workspace may not be built from until an Approved Decision record
  promotes it out of the gate.
- Gating is structural, not stylistic: the gate is asserted by test on every
  workspace panel.
- Showing a future workspace is not a roadmap commitment, a date, or a promise
  to a district.
