# Jurisdiction scope

## In scope

| Jurisdiction | Reason |
|---|---|
| US-FEDERAL | Education privacy, children's privacy, accessibility and federal procurement conditions reach any US education technology developer or its customers. |
| US-MA | Massachusetts is the stated first market. Several Massachusetts obligations bind a vendor directly rather than through a district. |
| NOT-JURISDICTIONAL | Voluntary standards and industry templates that carry no legal force but shape procurement. |

## The direct-versus-indirect distinction

This is the single most consequential structural finding of WP-038, and the register keeps the two
bases separate throughout.

**Binds a district, reaches SSLS only by contract.** FERPA, IDEA confidentiality, PPRA, 603 CMR
23.00, M.G.L. c. 71, ADA Title II and the HHS Section 504 rule all name the educational agency or
public entity as the regulated party. SSLS acquires duties under these regimes through a data-sharing
or procurement agreement, not by operation of law.

**Binds SSLS directly.** M.G.L. c. 93H, 201 CMR 17.00 and M.G.L. c. 93I attach to any person that
owns, licenses, receives, stores, maintains, processes or disposes of a Massachusetts resident's
personal information, independently of any district relationship. Massachusetts consumer protection
law, as read by the Attorney General's AI advisory, attaches to public representations now.

The practical consequence is recorded at OBL-009: the first legally binding obligation on SSLS is
more likely to arrive through operator payroll or payment data than through student data, because
the Massachusetts definition of personal information is narrow and does not require any student
relationship.

## Definitional non-equivalence

Personal information under c. 93H and 201 CMR 17.00 means a name combined with a Social Security
number, driver's licence or state ID number, or financial account number. Education-record data
under FERPA and 603 CMR 23.00 is far broader and usually contains none of those elements. A record
can be fully covered by one regime and entirely outside the other. The registers never merge them.
See CNF-003.
