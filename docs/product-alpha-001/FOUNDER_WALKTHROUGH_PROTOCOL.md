# Product Alpha 001 — founder/operator walkthrough protocol

**Status: NOT EXECUTED.** This document is a non-authoritative protocol. It is
**not** evidence, **not** a checkpoint, **not** usability validation, and **not**
an accessibility, educational, or conformance method. It binds nothing, closes
nothing, and authorizes nothing. Running it produces observations that an owner
may later choose to record; it does not by itself create a record.

Controlling decision for the increment it observes: `DEC-023`.

## 1. Purpose

Make the recorded next defensible owner action operational: walk the implemented
Product Alpha 001 loop once, as the founder/operator, and write down what was
confusing, slow, or missing — so the owner can decide between a bounded product
revision and a separately authorized TTR-001 audit.

## 2. Bound

- one session, one fictional learner, one goal, one complete loop;
- the synthetic seed data only — no real learner, district, or personal data may
  be entered at any point;
- the two authorized routes, `/learners` and `/learners/$learnerId`;
- one browser, local storage only; nothing is shared, synced, or published.

## 3. Steps

1. Start from an empty learner list and load the synthetic sample data.
2. Open one fictional learner that has a focus goal and no baseline.
3. Record a baseline observation against the focus goal.
4. Plan a support action that cites that observation.
5. Mark the support completed.
6. Record a follow-up observation after completion.
7. Record the result of the follow-up.
8. Read the timeline and the deterministic next-step projection.
9. Export the document, reload the page, and confirm the loop is still there.

## 4. What to record while walking

For each step, note only what was observed:

- **elapsed time** for the step, and for the whole loop;
- **confusion points** — where the next control was not obvious;
- **dead ends** — where no next action was available or the path required
  going backwards;
- **rationale comprehension** — whether the stated reason for the next step, and
  the records it cited, were understandable without reading the code;
- **missing information** — what the operator wanted to see and could not.

## 5. What this protocol may not be used to claim

Completing this walkthrough does not establish usability validation, usefulness,
workflow stability, learning impact, educational validity, accessibility
conformance, privacy or security certification, production, pilot or district
readiness, release, or any independent validation. It does not authorize the
TTR-001 audit, any successor increment, or any expansion of Product Alpha 001.
