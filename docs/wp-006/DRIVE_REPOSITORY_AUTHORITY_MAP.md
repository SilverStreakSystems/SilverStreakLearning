implement, release, or create public-claim authority.

## Classification legend

- **AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE** — Drive holds the controlling copy; the repository may mirror it.
- **REPOSITORY AUTHORITATIVE** — executable or code-bound truth; the repository controls the fact.
- **CONTROL INDEX** — navigation and reconciliation aid; creates no authority.
- **RESEARCH / REFERENCE** — may inform future work; cannot authorize current implementation.
- **HISTORICAL EVIDENCE** — preserved for provenance only.
- **CANDIDATE** — pending explicit promotion.
