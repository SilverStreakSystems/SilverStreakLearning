## Placement rules

- A capability belongs to the domain that would be **accountable** for it, not
  the domain that consumes it.
- Cross-cutting obligations (accessibility, privacy, evidence discipline) are
  owned once and constrain everywhere. They are not duplicated per domain.
- A capability that could sit in two domains is placed where its stop authority
  lives, and the other domain is recorded under supporting functions.

## Known weaknesses in v0.1

- D12 is the thinnest domain and may be under-specified.
- The boundary between D08 and D09 is judgment-based and may not survive review.
- Domain counts are near-uniform, which may reflect authoring habit rather than
  actual organizational weight. Recorded as an open question.
