# Open Questions — Capability Architecture v0.1

Recorded honestly rather than resolved by assertion.

1. Is 129 the right granularity, or does it over-specify an organization that
   currently has one person?
2. Domain counts are near-uniform. Does that reflect real organizational weight
   or the habit of the author?
3. Is the D08 / D09 boundary (market versus service delivery) durable, or will
   it collapse at first contract?
4. Should customer success, account management, and renewals (merged as
   CAP-D09-008 in NORM-015) split again at S4?
5. Who can hold independent assurance capabilities before SSLS can pay for
   external specialists? Currently: nobody, which means those capabilities are
   absent rather than covered.
6. Is "Assured" maturity reachable at all without a paying district, or is it
   structurally gated behind revenue?
7. Are the five trigger gates sufficient, or is a separate gate needed for
   handling special education and protected-group data specifically?
8. The dependency set is 38 edges and is certainly incomplete. What is the
   method for finding missing edges rather than adding them ad hoc?
9. How is drift between this registry and actual practice detected once practice
   exists? CAP-D12-005 names the ability; no method is defined.
10. What evidence would justify promoting v0.1 from Research / Candidate to an
    Accepted Decision, and who is qualified to review it?

Each question is a gap in knowledge, not a task list. Answering them requires
decisions and evidence, not further implementation.
