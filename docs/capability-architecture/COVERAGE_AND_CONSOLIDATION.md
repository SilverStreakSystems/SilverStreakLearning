# Coverage and Consolidation

Coverage records how a capability may legitimately be held **today**, at S0.

## Coverage modes

- Founder
- Combined internal role
- Dedicated internal role
- External specialist
- Independent external review
- Automated / deterministic system support
- Not yet required

## Rules

- Consolidation is allowed by default. A small organization holding several
  capabilities in one role is normal and is not a defect.
- Consolidation is forbidden where independence is Required, and where a record
  names the combination under `incompatibleCombinations`.
- "Not yet required" is a legitimate answer and must be stated rather than left
  blank.

## The founder single point of failure

At S0 the founder covers most capabilities. This is recorded explicitly in
NORM-014 with resolution state Conflicted. The registry does not present founder
coverage as resolution; it presents it as concentration risk that later stages
must remove.

Viewable at `/capabilities?view=coverage`.
